# Workplace monitoring notice

Reviewed against official sources on 3 October 2026. This document describes product controls; it is not a determination that an organisation's monitoring is lawful.

Before enabling monitoring, the organisation administrator must acknowledge responsibility for written notice, a valid legal basis, employee consent where required, proportionality, access restrictions and retention. Employees must actively accept the current monitoring policy in the web app. A checkbox alone does not establish legal compliance.

The notice explains screen sharing, optional webcam (off by default), focused application names and window titles, flag-triggered clips with a short pre-roll, who may view them, the retention period, work hours and how to pause. Employees can see their own flags. Policy changes invalidate prior consent. Pause stays available and is recorded for managers; it must not be used as automatic evidence of misconduct. No keystroke logging, passwords, hidden capture, audio capture or off-hours monitoring is provided. Do not enter private material in a shared screen/window title.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the notified Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 use phased commencement. The Rules notification states different commencement periods; organisations must check the provisions currently in force, applicable employment/sector laws and notice requirements rather than assume every provision began on publication. Student accounts involve minors and require additional institutional/guardian protections; account creation remains under teachers' control and no student photographs are collected.

In the EU, the GDPR requires a lawful basis and transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, security and retention limits. Employee consent can be invalid where the employment relationship prevents a freely given choice. Assess lawful basis with appropriate advice, whether a DPIA is required, and local employment/works-council rules. Plinth's active consent control remains mandatory even if a different lawful basis applies. Obtain the relevant notices/agreements and provide an alternative process where required.

Each organisation acts as the controller/data fiduciary for its use and is responsible for lawful configuration. Platform administrators must maintain processor terms, deletion/export processes and incident response. Default recording retention is 30 days and can only be shortened or increased within the platform maximum. Only authorised managers/teachers can view their own tenant's streams and clips. Files require channel membership; all downloads expire. Org suspension stops monitoring. Data exports/deletion require administrator access and explicit confirmation of the organisation name.
